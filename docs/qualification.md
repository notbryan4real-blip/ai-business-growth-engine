# Prospect qualification

The qualified queue is intentionally **sales-oriented**, not simply a list of the
businesses with the most reviews.

## Current signals

- No dedicated official website: highest-priority website opportunity.
- Legacy HTTP or clearly old web footprint: strong website-rebuild signal.
- High-value service category: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and similar
  services have meaningful customer lifetime value and recurring operational work.
- Strong ratings/review volume: indicates that a business may already have
  enough demand to justify better conversion infrastructure.
- Multi-service or multi-area operation: increases the value of lead routing,
  receptionist, scheduling, follow-up, CRM, and review automation.

## Score interpretation

- **70+ / A:** immediate website/outreach candidate.
- **55-69 / A:** strong candidate; inspect the site manually before outreach.
- **40-54 / B:** better fit for an automation/lead-conversion pitch or a site
  refresh than a simple no-website sale.
- **<40:** keep in the database, but do not prioritize until additional signals
  are found.

The scores are a screening heuristic, not a claim about the business's revenue,
intent to buy, or website performance. Website quality should be re-scored after
a real page-speed/mobile/UX audit.
