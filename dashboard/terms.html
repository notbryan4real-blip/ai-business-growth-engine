Purpose and limitations

Growth Engine is an operational workspace for researching business opportunities, preparing marketing drafts, and tracking prospect and revenue information. Scores are screening heuristics, not probabilities of a sale or guarantees of business results. AI-generated content can be inaccurate and must be checked against reliable evidence.

Do not use it to harass recipients, evade opt-outs, fabricate claims, or submit sensitive personal information that is not needed for the task.

Use of Google Maps Platform content is subject to the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service and the Places API policies. Google Places results are shown temporarily with attribution; the backend retains only place IDs. Other providers may process content submitted to their services under their own terms.

Human review and external actions

Generated proposals and outreach drafts are not sent automatically. Users are responsible for verifying facts, obtaining required approvals, and ensuring communications and customer-site changes are authorized.

The application is provided on an as-available basis. Browser-local records may be lost if browser data is cleared or the device changes. Export backups where available. The current MVP does not promise durable storage, uninterrupted availability, or any particular business outcome.
